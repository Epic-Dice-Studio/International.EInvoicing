Expected content: the XSD and Schematron of the five profiles (MINIMUM, BASIC WL, BASIC, EN 16931, EXTENDED)
and the official sample invoices.

The specification PDF must be downloaded from FNFE-MPE and is **not** committed. `build/fetch-specs.sh`
prints the URL and the target path.

Note that Factur-X and ZUGFeRD are the same standard published by two organisations: profile identifiers
differ but the XML is identical.
